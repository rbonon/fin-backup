fin-backup privacy policy

fin-backup is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • It accesses Google Drive only with the drive.file permission, limited to files it creates.
  • Files are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload. No one else, including the author of this page, can read them.
  • No personal data is collected, stored on any server, shared, or sold. There is no server; everything runs on the owner's computer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
